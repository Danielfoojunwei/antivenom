AntiVenom: Question-Scoped False Claims as
Portable Antibodies Against Swarm Memory Poisoning

One agent catches a poisoned record. Instead of sharing where it is stored, it shares what it claims, and every agent in the swarm can find and remove the poison in its own memory.
